Cloud Snooze

Intelligent infra scaling · Cloud cost control

In Progress
Python GCP Azure Kubernetes Terraform FinOps Automation

Problem: Cloud resources keep running at full capacity during off-peak hours — idle VMs, scaled-out clusters, and always-on databases drain budget around the clock even when traffic has cooled off.

A cloud-native scheduler that monitors traffic signals and resource utilisation across GCP and Azure, then intelligently scales down VMs, GKE node pools, and managed databases during quiet windows — and pre-emptively scales them back up ahead of demand.

  • Unified policy engine covering VMs, GKE clusters, and managed databases across cloud providers.
  • Pre-emptive scale-up driven by predicted traffic ramp — not reactive to spikes.
  • Configurable cool-off thresholds, exclusion rules, and schedule overrides per resource type.
